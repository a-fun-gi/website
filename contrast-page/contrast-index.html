c o n t r a s t

Contrast is a monochrome metroidvania involving light, shadows, and getting lost in the dark built in Godot. Contrast is still under development, and any contributions are welcome! For now, you can download a demo version of the game and play it, but please be aware that it is still in development and may contain bugs or unfinished features.
